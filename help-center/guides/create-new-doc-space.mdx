---
title: Create a New Documentation Space
description: Guide to starting a new doc space on Neutrino.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Dashboard">
    Login at `https://dashboard.neutrino.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Documentation Space">
    Locate and click the **“+ New Documentation Space”** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill Out Details">
    Enter your space’s name and description. Choose visibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Collaborators">
    Add emails of teammates to invite them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Click **Create**. Your space is ready!
  </Step>
</Steps>
