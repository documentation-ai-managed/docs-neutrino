---
title: Cannot Access Dashboard
description: Fix login or loading issues with the Neutrino dashboard.
---

## Issue

- Dashboard fails to load
- Login page keeps reloading

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check internet connection">
    Ensure your device is connected to the web.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    Try clearing cookies and reloading the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use a supported browser">
    Neutrino works best with latest Chrome, Firefox, or Edge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    If still blocked, email support@neutrino.com with a screenshot.
  </Step>
</Steps>
